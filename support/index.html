Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? inside Neutrino to see this list at any time.

J KMove down and up. Hold Shift to select several, Option to carry a task along.
CWrite a new task. Inside a task it adds subtasks, and Enter starts the next one.
EMark done. Finished tasks move to the Archive tab.
ZUndo. Shift + Z redoes.
HSet a due date in plain words: tomorrow, 10am thu, next week, 8/10, 3 days.
LAdd or remove a label. Each label becomes a tab along the top.
D SIndent and outdent, to nest a task under the one above.
EnterOpen a task to add a description and subtasks. Esc goes back.
TabSwitch tabs.
⌘ KSearch every task and every command.

Where is my list saved?

As a single markdown file called todo.md. Press ⌘ K and choose Show the tasks file to see it in Finder.

How do I use it on more than one Mac?

Press ⌘ K, choose Sync with Google Drive, and pick a folder that syncs between your Macs, such as one in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. Do the same on the other Mac and both will share one list.

I finished a task by mistake

Press Z straight away to undo, or open the Archive tab, select the task and press E to bring it back.
