Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? inside Neutrino to see every shortcut at any time.

J KMove down and up. Hold Shift to select several, Option to carry something along.
CWrite a new task or note. Inside a task it adds subtasks, and Enter starts the next one.
EMark a task done, or archive a note. Finished tasks move to the archive.
ZUndo. Shift + Z redoes.
HSet a due date in plain words: tomorrow, 10am thu, next week, 8/10, 3 days.
RSnooze a task until later, or repeat it: every other sunday, every day at 5pm, every month.
LAdd or remove a label. Each label gets a tab of its own.
D SIndent and outdent, to nest a task or note under the one above.
XFold or unfold every task's subtasks. In Notes, expand or collapse every note.
EnterOpen a task or note. Esc goes back.
TIn Notes, send a note, or one paragraph of it, to Tasks.
⌘ OOpen a link, or attach one, even a plain address like google.com.
⌘ C ⌘ VCopy and paste. ⌘ X cuts, and text pasted from elsewhere keeps its outline.
G T G NGo to Tasks, or to Notes.
G AEvery open task, or in Notes every note, archived ones too.
G HEverything with a due date, day by day.
G REverything snoozed.
G LLabels: rename or merge them, make one permanent, and set the order of your tabs.
G OEvery link, each with a title of its own.
G EThe archive.
TabNext tab. Shift + Tab goes back.
⌘ KSearch every task, note, link and command.

Where are my tasks and notes saved?

In two markdown files side by side: neutrino-tasks.md and neutrino-notes.md, in the Neutrino folder in your Documents unless you've moved them. Press ⌘ K and choose Show the tasks file or Show the notes file to see them in Finder.

How do I use it on more than one Mac?

Choose File > Move Tasks and Notes… and pick a folder that syncs between your Macs, such as one in Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox. On the other Mac, choose File > Open Tasks or Notes File… and pick the same files. If both Macs change a file, Neutrino merges the changes.

I finished a task by mistake

Press Z straight away to undo, or press G E for the archive, select the task and press E to bring it back.
